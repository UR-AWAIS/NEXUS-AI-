01 — the legend

Raised under the floorboards of an old dojo

He was never named for a color or a brother. He was named for what he carries — a shell that has taken every blow meant for someone slower, and a will that never once asked to trade it in. Ronin Shell moves the way old bamboo moves: bent by every storm, broken by none.

02 — the arsenal

What he trained with, not what he was given

Twin Sai

Close-range, defensive first — built to catch a blade, not just meet it.

Bo Staff

Distance and patience. The weapon of someone willing to wait you out.

Shell Stance

No weapon at all — just the discipline to know when not to draw one.

03 — the dojo

Trains alone. Fights for the four who don't.

Every session starts the same way: lights low, shell to the wall, breath counted in fours. Not for show — the dojo has no audience. Just the floor, the staff, and whatever version of himself showed up that day.
